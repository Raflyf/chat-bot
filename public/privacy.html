Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Overview

FreeAIBot ("we", "our", or "the service") is an automated artificial intelligence assistant accessible via messaging platforms including Telegram and WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy outlines how user data and messages are handled when interacting with our bot.

2. Information We Collect

When you send messages to FreeAIBot, we process:

  • Message Content: Text queries and media you send to receive answers from the AI assistant.
  • Identifiers: Your platform user ID or phone number to maintain session state and conversation memory.
  • Timestamps: Times of interaction for rate limiting and service stability.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected is used strictly to:

  • Generate responses to your queries using artificial intelligence models.
  • Maintain conversational context for ongoing discussions.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, and maintain service security.

4. Data Sharing and Retention

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data is processed through secure serverless functions and trusted AI providers solely to complete your requested actions.

5. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users have the right to request deletion of their conversation history and stored session data at any time by contacting the bot administrator or issuing a reset command.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via the official repository or support channel.
